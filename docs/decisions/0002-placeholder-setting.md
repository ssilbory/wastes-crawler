# 0002: Placeholder setting until the content phase

Status: accepted, 2026-10-03

## Context

The plan left the setting open until Phase 6: regions, factions, the Dark Lord's legacy, and towns. Phase 6 builds towns and NPCs, but the engine work doesn't depend on what the world is.

## Decision

- Phases 6 to 11 use the Borderlands test region from Phase 5 and its three towns, with generic names, services, and sample NPCs.
- The real setting is decided in Phase 12, Content and campaign, and replaces the placeholder content then.

## Consequences

- No engine work waits on worldbuilding. Town, NPC, and quest content stays small and replaceable.
- Placeholder content must stay generic, so it doesn't harden into the setting by accident.

## Update, 2026-10-03

The setting is now written: The Dark Lands, in [docs/setting.md](../setting.md). It still applies only in Phase 12; Phases 9 to 11 keep the placeholder content. Phase 12 reskins towns, monsters, dungeons, quests, and captives to fit it, and turns its systems (non-human playable peoples, beastman source animals, standing orders, bound agents, flesh-crafting) into rules and content.
